## 🚫 Blacklist of Forbidden Elements
To maintain artistic integrity and sovereign precision, VVE prohibits:
- **Structural layout in shortcuts** (flex, grid, h-screen). Layout must be local to the page.
- **Large external margins** in components (mb-10+). Spacing is contextual.
- **Side crescents** for active states (replace with Precision Sight).
- **Rounded-xl or larger** (limit to technical radii of 2px to 6px).
